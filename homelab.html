THE HOMELAB

Proxmox · VLANs · Firewall · VPN · Logging

WHY IT EXISTS

Self-initiated, built outside coursework, running since early 2025. The goal: a production-style environment where breaking things is free and every service is something I configured, secured, and documented myself.

NETWORK MAP

Traffic between VLANs is deny-by-default; only the specific flows I need are allowed through firewall rules. Management interfaces are reachable only from the MGMT VLAN.

WHAT'S RUNNING

  • Proxmox VE — hypervisor hosting the Linux VM fleet.
  • Firewall — inter-VLAN policy, NAT, and the VPN endpoint for remote access.
  • Centralized logging — syslog from every VM and network device into one place.
  • Backups — scheduled VM snapshots and off-box copies, restore-tested.
  • Lab VLAN — isolated space for CCDC prep, malware-free attack/defense drills, and cert studying.

LESSONS THAT STUCK

  • Segmentation is cheap before you build and expensive after — VLAN design came first, services second.
  • A backup that hasn't been restored is a rumor. Restore tests are on the schedule, not "someday."
  • Centralized logs turned debugging from guessing into reading.
